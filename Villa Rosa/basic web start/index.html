Welcome to Villa Rosa

A much-loved family home, right on the water.

Villa Rosa sits directly on the seafront in Viganj, on the Pelješac Peninsula — a hand-built stone house, not a hotel, filled with the character of a real family home. The pool is private, the terrace looks straight out to Korčula, and the village is close enough to walk to for dinner.

Mornings start with a swim off the terrace. Evenings end with something cold in hand, watching the light change over the island. In between, there's very little you need to do at all.

Viganj & Pelješac

A windsurfing village, a wine peninsula, an island across the water.

Viganj is a small seafront village on the Pelješac Peninsula, known across Croatia for its wind — this is windsurfing and kitesurfing country. Beyond the village: vineyards, quiet beaches, and Korčula Old Town just a short ferry ride away.
