10/10 Excellent

“As for the pool, it made our holiday.”

What a wonderful holiday we've just had! Our party of four adults and four teenagers had a great time and it was all thanks to the outstanding accommodation in Viganj. The large lounge and kitchen area fitted everyone in comfortably, with a large table available for dining or card/board games.

Read full review →Show less

There were also dining tables at the front of the house and at the rear, where a perfect sheltered area meant breakfasts and lunches could be had without risks from the sun. The kitchen had a coffee maker (Nespresso type) and an Italian stove top one. The hob and oven were sizeable, to allow serious cooking. There were easily enough plates, cups and tumblers to survive beyond the point of a full dishwasher. The washing machine, second fridge/freezer and microwave were ideal.

The bedrooms were great for our group, with one family going downstairs, the other upstairs. Each room had air conditioning, so sleeping was easy. Toilets and showers were clean and suitably roomy. As for the pool, it made our holiday, as the youngsters - as well as the adults - played in it for hours. Water temperature was great and the size was adequate for all eight of us to have fun.

Viganj is quite a distance from Dubrovnik, so be prepared for a couple of hours of driving, or more, but it's well served with a supermarket, two bakeries, and several excellent restaurants & bars. You will not go hungry!

Overall, I can only thoroughly recommend this villa for its outstanding facilities and the great locale.

Come and leave one of your own.
