Korčula, Pelješac & beyond

Korčula Island

A medieval old town, right across the water.

The ferry from Viganj crosses to Korčula in around ten minutes, or it's roughly a 30-minute drive around via Orebić. Korčula Old Town — said to be the birthplace of Marco Polo — is well worth the trip, with its walled streets, waterfront restaurants and the Korčula Town and Marco Polo museums.
