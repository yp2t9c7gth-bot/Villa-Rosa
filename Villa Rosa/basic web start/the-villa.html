The Villa

Stone walls. Wooden beams. Room to breathe.

The house

A real home, restored with care.

Villa Rosa combines hand-carved stonework and rich wooden details with fresh, modern touches. It's a house built for comfort and connection — every corner reflects the beauty and character of Viganj, right down to the stone table on the terrace where most meals happen.

The upstairs bathrooms and the kitchen were fully refurbished in 2025, with a brand-new oven and hob, so the practical side of a family holiday is as easy as the rest of it.

Pool & terrace

Private pool. Panoramic sea views.

The pool sits in its own private courtyard, framed by an authentic stone wall, with sunbeds and all-day sun. Out front, the terrace looks straight over the Adriatic — dine al fresco at the large stone table and watch the water sparkle towards Korčula.

Outdoor living

BBQ, lounge seating, soft evening light.

Open-air BBQ, comfortable lounge seating and dining tables make the outdoors the real living room at Villa Rosa. As the sun goes down, soft lighting takes over and dinner moves outside.
